About Us

Kard is a leading Saudi company specialized in tailoring clothes and providing mobile tailoring services to our customers wherever they are. We are distinguished by a wide variety of fabrics and the latest cutting and sewing machine technology.

نفخر في تقديم خدماتنا لعملائنا في الوسطى والغربية والشرقية، ونطمح في تقديمها على مستوى المملكة العربية السعودية بالكامل.

We are proud to provide our services to customers in the central, western and eastern regions of Saudi Arabia, and we aspire to provide them throughout the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
